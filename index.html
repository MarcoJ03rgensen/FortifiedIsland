About Bornholm Graves Explorer

Dataset Origins & Harmonization

This application dynamically parses and visualizes bornholm_graves_master.csv (222 archaeological sites), synthesized from:

  • Fund og Fortidsminder (Slots- og Kulturstyrelsen) baseline GIS registry.
  • Lutz, L. H. (2010): Die Insel in der Mitte: Bornholm im 2.–4. Jahrhundert (39 primary catalogue sites and 23 supplemental sites).
  • Jørgensen, L. (1985): Gravpladser og samfundsforhold på Bornholm i perioden 175–975 e.Kr. (18 supplementary regional sites and long-term continuity data).
  • Foss, P. (1989): Baunegård - grave fra yngre romersk og ældre germansk jernalder.
  • Nielsen, F. O. (2026): Oversigt over gravpladser fra ældre jernalder på Bornholm (Bornholms Museum unpublished research archive / registrant).

Live Master CSV Synchronization

Any modifications saved directly into bornholm_graves_master.csv are automatically detected and reloaded into the visualizer in real time.

Period Classifications

  • Pre-Roman Iron Age: Førromersk jernalder (~500 BC – 1 AD)
  • Early Roman Iron Age: Ældre romersk jernalder (~1 – 160 AD)
  • Late Roman Iron Age: Yngre romersk jernalder (~160 – 375/400 AD)
  • Early Germanic Iron Age / Migration Period: Folkevandringstid (~375/400 – 550 AD)
  • Late Germanic & Viking Age: Merovingertid / Vendeltid & Vikingetid (~550 – 1050 AD)
